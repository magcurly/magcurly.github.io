## 12-06-2026

Today I received news that my paper has been principally accepted by *Nature Communications*. I should feel elated. And somewhere beneath everything, maybe I do — a little.

But mostly I feel released.

This project started in May 2023 with genuine excitement. This is the first time I can get in touch with new extreme environment. I was enthusiastic when I took that job.

Then came the collaboration. I won't go into detail, but the experience wore me down in ways that are hard to articulate. I felt being bullied by the collaborator. First, I was forced to drink alcohol which I am really unhappy about since I am really worried about getting into allergy. Afterwards, I was forced to explain the result in a way I was not showing my consent with my expertise perspective. The final paper — the one that got accepted — is not the paper I set out to write. It's not something I feel I can fully call mine. Although most of the results remained as the manuscript I wrote in 2023, how they were described is not the way I want.  

Maybe that's okay. Maybe this is just what finishing things sometimes looks like — not a triumphant finish line, but a quiet, complicated exhale. The work is done. It will reach people. And I can move on to something that feels like mine again.

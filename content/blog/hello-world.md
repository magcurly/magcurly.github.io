Welcome to my new website! I've rebuilt it using [Zola](https://www.getzola.org/), a blazing-fast static site generator written in Rust.

## Why Zola?

- **Fast**: Built in Rust, Zola compiles sites in milliseconds
- **Simple**: No dependencies, single binary, straightforward templating
- **Flexible**: Sass compilation, syntax highlighting, and feeds built-in
